Making it practical

Two shortcuts

Warm start

Study your own best attempts first

Before the main practice, the model tries each task many times. We keep only the successful attempts and train it to repeat them.

32 tries per task → keep the successes → train on those

Full name: rejection-sampling fine-tuning.

A small add-on (LoRA)

Train a small add-on, not the whole model

The original model stays frozen. Training changes a small add-on beside it: under 2% of the model’s size, a 260 MB file.

Dark green: the add-on, about 1 of every 50 parts. Grey: the frozen model.

The warm start moves tasks from almost never solved toward sometimes solved, which is where the practice rounds can learn. The add-on, called LoRA (low-rank adaptation), is why all of this fits on one rented graphics card: only a small file changes, and it is cheap to copy after every round.
